# What is Denshattack?

Denshattack is a 2026 indie grind-fiction game, where players take to the rails in a train to flip, trick, and crash their way through Japan, taking down an evil Megacorporation while they're at it. This manual serves as a stop-gap, and proof of concept of an apworld until a proper implementation can be made.

# Requirements

This manual was designed with allowing both new save files and 100% saves to be used, depending on player preference. Both approaches have various pros and cons: 100% savefiles may use different trains as they are sent, but cannot follow shop progression as easily and don't benefit from in-game Dare marking, and 0% savefiles have the inverse benefits (can use shops and Dare marking, but not items as they are sent as easily)

# How does Randomization work?

All access to Trains, Tricks, and Regions is randomized by default. Spray Can, Gear, and Film Roll collectibles are also randomized, and Recieving certain amounts of each gates shops and Fanzine checks. Movement Randomization is an option, which further gates access to various levels. Checks are included for Clearing levels, Gold, Silver and Bronze medals for both Score and Time category, and a Daresanity option for Every Dare in the game. If you use Deathlink, it's recommended to exclude No Crash Dares from this category. To win the game and beat the randomizer, Collect enough Progressive regions and mechanics to progress through the game, and Clear level 9-2-B.

# Progressive Items

This Denshattack manual features 2 progressive items, Tricks and Regions. Progressive Region gates your access to the various levels of the game in addition to movement mechanics, since the latter is optional. The Region is found as the first number in each level's name, or in the top left of the world map. (3-1-1 is the first level in Region 3 (Shikoku), which needs 2 Progressive Region items to access).

Progressive Trick gates your ability to perform tricks. At 0 Progressive Trick items recieved, you are NOT able to perform ANY tricks at all. This increases with each Progressive Trick recieved, with 1 item allowing Basic Tricks, progressing through each category in the Tricktionary up to allowing all Tricks at 4 items recieved. Tricks which require you to be currently doing a specific movement mechanic, such as Grind Tricks, are their own item if movement is randomized, and freely usable if not.

* There is ONE EXCEPTION for the use of Progressive Trick. For the purpose of clearing level 1-1-2, you may perform tricks following the direction of the tutorial while on the conveyor rail. Afterwards, Arial Transfers count as tricks to satisfy the "Perform 5 Tricks" Requirement. This is to prevent sphere 1 consisting of exactly 1 check, for clearing 1-1-1.

# About Death

This manual has Death Link. Recieving a Death means you should crash your train as soon as possible, WITHOUT banking any accumulated score. This can usually be accomplished by immediately inputting a fully charged jump to derail yourself, but may need more improvisation. You should send a Death when you crash your train, but it is recommended to be more discriminating. I used a mental Deathlink Amnesty, sending Death only every 5 crashes, but you could just as easily come up with other conditions, such as sending only if you crash with a high combo.
